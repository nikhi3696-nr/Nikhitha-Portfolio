Hi, I'm Nikki — a B2B marketing professional with 7+ years of experience building brands and generating demand for complex, technical businesses.

Before Entrust, I worked at international agency KRDS across Singapore and UAE, on accounts including BMW, The Body Shop, Coca-Cola, and Singapore Zoo. I hold an MBA from the University of Chester and am an Associate Member of CIM.
